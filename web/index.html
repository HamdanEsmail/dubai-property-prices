How to read this

Method: a gradient-boosting model predicts the log price per square metre from community, type, rooms, size, off-plan status and nearby landmarks; it was fitted on January to August 2026 and checked on September to October (…).

Prices are registered sale values, not asking prices. Bulk portfolio deals, land, offices and implausible entries are removed before anything is computed. The estimate is a guide, not a valuation.

Data: Dubai Land Department open data (Transactions), used under the Dubai Open Data Licence. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Dubai Land Department or the Government of Dubai.
